Jalanna

Privacy policy

Draft for testing (15 November 2026). To be reviewed by a lawyer for Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 before Jalanna is offered to the public. Contact details below are to be filled in.

What stays on your phone only

  • Home, Work, recent and saved places, planned drives and where you parked.
  • Your settings, including language and route options.
  • Your location while you navigate. Routes are calculated by sending only the start, stops and destination to the routing server.

What the Jalanna server receives

  • An anonymous device id. The app registers once and receives a random token. No name, email or phone number. The server stores only a fingerprint (hash) of the token.
  • Reports you make (type, place, time, optional detail) and your "still there" votes and abuse flags. Reports are public on the map without any name.
  • An optional nickname for your profile, if you set one.
  • Searches: what you type and your approximate position go to the place search service (Photon, with OpenStreetMap data) and, when the Jalanna server is used, to that server to search Overture Maps places and places drivers added. Searches are not stored.
  • Places you add ("Add a missing place"): name, kind, optional note and location, linked to your anonymous device id. Only you see them until a moderator approves them; then they appear in everyone's search without your name. Rejected places are deleted after 90 days.
  • Share my drive, only when you tap Share: destination, arrival time and position, visible to anyone with the link, until you stop, 30 minutes after arrival, or after 12 hours. Not stored on disk. You see how many people have the link open; each open page is counted with a random number it makes up, and nothing about viewers is kept.
  • Live traffic, only if you turn on "Help with live traffic": speed and position in 2-minute pieces with no device id, nothing from the first and last 500 m of a trip. The server matches each piece to roads, keeps average road speeds only, and does not store the points.
  • Place searches go to the search service (Photon) and map pictures are loaded from the map tile server.

How long data is kept

  • Reports, with their votes and flags, are deleted 90 days after they expire.
  • Average road speeds are kept for 12 months (for typical traffic by time of day). They contain no information about drivers.
  • The moderation log (ids and actions only) is kept for 12 months.

Your choices

  • See your data: Settings → Privacy → Export my data.
  • Delete your data: Settings → Privacy → Delete my data removes your reports, votes, flags, added places, nickname and device from the server and clears the app.
  • Stop live traffic at any time in Settings.
